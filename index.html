    About This Page

    This is a simple Progressive Web App (PWA) built with plain HTML, CSS, and JS. It fetches real-time data for currency rates from the Central Bank of Russia, weather based on your location (or Moscow as fallback), and news from RIA.ru RSS. It also includes a fun dots animation section. The app can be installed on your device for offline access (basic caching of static files).
